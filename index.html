I'm Shahin Biswas Milon, a Lead Generation & AI Automation Specialist based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I help sales, recruiting and research teams get accurate data without the manual grind.

My work sits where data engineering meets automation: Python and Playwright scrapers that pull clean, structured data from complex websites; enrichment and validation pipelines that make every record outreach-ready; and n8n + LLM agents that answer customers, triage inboxes and run back-office tasks on their own.

I care about accuracy as much as speed — every dataset is QC-checked, every figure traceable to its source, and every workflow built to keep running without me. If it's repetitive, I automate it. If it lives on the web, I can extract it.
